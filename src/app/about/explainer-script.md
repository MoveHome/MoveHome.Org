# MoveHome.org Explainer Video Script: "The Open Property Commons"

**Target Audience:** Tenants, Independent Landlords, Homeowners, and Local Estate Agents.
**Tone:** Warm, trustworthy, empowering, non-technical, and community-first.
**Format:** A friendly 2-minute whiteboard/slideshow or narrated explainer video.

---

### [SCENE 1: THE TOLL ROAD]
**Visual:** A friendly cartoon character (representing a local independent landlord or tenant) walking down a road, only to hit a massive, high-tech toll gate labeled "Incumbent Portals" that demands a bag of money just to pass.

**Narrator (Voiceover):** 
Finding a home or renting out a property shouldn't feel like driving on an endless toll road. But today, a few giant, centralized portals stand in the middle of our housing market. They charge estate agents and landlords thousands of pounds in subscription fees just to show listings to the public. 

**Text on Screen:** Portal Tolls = High Listing Fees $\rightarrow$ Passed down to Landlords $\rightarrow$ Higher Rents for Tenants.

**Narrator:**
These tolls don’t stay with the agents—they get passed down to landlords, which ultimately drives up rents and house prices for everyone. Our property listings—which represent our homes and lives—are locked inside private, corporate silos.

---

### [SCENE 2: THE PARADIGM SHIFT — MOVEHOME.ORG]
**Visual:** The toll gate crumbles, and the path transforms into a wide, beautiful public park where people are handshaking directly. A warm blue house logo appears with the name **MoveHome.org**.

**Narrator:**
It’s time to take our housing market back. Welcome to **MoveHome.org**—the UK’s first completely free, open, and not-for-profit property listings directory. 

**Text on Screen:** MoveHome.org: 100% Free to List, 100% Free to Search, Governed by MoveHome Foundation CIC.

**Narrator:**
MoveHome is not a commercial corporation out to squeeze profit from your rent. It is a public utility governed by the **MoveHome Foundation CIC**—a legally asset-locked, non-profit Community Interest Company. Our mission is simple: to make the infrastructure of property search a public commons, not a toll road.

---

### [SCENE 3: HOW IT WORKS — AI-TO-AI PORTABILITY]
**Visual:** We see an Estate Agent's local office database shining, sending a small, structured envelope (labeled `raia.json`) through the air directly to the MoveHome.org search engine.

**Narrator:**
So, how does it work? It’s all powered by an open, cooperative language called the **RAIA Protocol**. 
Instead of typing listings manually into expensive, closed portals, estate agents simply publish their properties directly on their own websites in this open language. 

MoveHome’s automated system safely crawls the web, aggregates these free listings, and presents them in a beautiful, unified search engine for tenants and homebuyers. No middleman. No listing fees. No lead-generation charges.

---

### [SCENE 4: BIOMETRIC SAFETY & TOTAL DATA PRIVACY]
**Visual:** A shield pops up showing a clean, anonymous face-verification check, with a lock on a file labeled "Your Personal Data."

**Narrator:**
But we didn't just stop at free search. By removing the corporate middleman, we’ve built safety and privacy directly into the architecture:
*   **Biometric Safety:** MoveHome features cryptographic anti-subletting checks and ID verification (powered by Sumsub) to protect landlords against fraud and keep tenants safe.
*   **Absolute Privacy:** Your personal information is secured inside encrypted, local GDPR private enclaves. Your data is never sold, never leased, and never fed to commercial AI models.

---

### [SCENE 5: JOIN THE COMMONS]
**Visual:** A community of tenants, landlords, and independent estate agents smiling, holding a sign that says "Join the MoveHome Commons."

**Narrator:**
MoveHome.org is a cooperative ecosystem. By removing the gatekeepers, we return the value where it belongs: in your pocket, in your community, and in your home.
*   **Are you a Tenant or Buyer?** Search thousands of real, direct listings with zero spam.
*   **Are you a Landlord?** Protect your yield and secure your property with active rent-guarantees and biometric vetting.
*   **Are you an Estate Agent?** Save thousands in portal fees and list your properties for free.

**Narrator:**
Visit **MoveHome.org** today. Let’s build a fairer, more transparent, and sovereign property market—together. 

**Closing Screen:** Warm blue MoveHome.org logo with the subtext: *"The Open Property Commons. Governed by MoveHome Foundation CIC."*
